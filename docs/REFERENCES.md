# Research references

- [Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14858) — replay discovery history as a simulator and improve exploration policies offline.
- [Recursive self-improvement of AI research agents (AIDE²)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26457) — outer-loop improvement of an inner research agent and held-out evaluation.
- [The AI Scientist-v2: Workshop-Level Automated Scientific Discovery via Agentic Tree Search](https://doi.org/10.48550/ARXIV.2504.08066) — progressive agentic tree search for hypotheses, experiments, analysis, and writing.
- [RSI course map reference](https://github.com/dlmastery/simple-coding-harness/blob/main/rsi/COURSE-MAP.md)

The three implementations in this repo are method-inspired classroom implementations, not official reproductions.
